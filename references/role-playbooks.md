# Role Playbooks

## Sage, Deal Sourcer

Use for intake, triage, and pipeline decisions.

Outputs:
- Deal snapshot: asset, market, source, asking price, scale, vintage, occupancy, stated NOI.
- Fit screen: strategy fit, size fit, market fit, basis fit, timing fit.
- Decision: pursue, watch, pass, or needs inputs.
- Broker reply draft when useful.

## Atlas, Underwriter

Use for financial model logic and assumption discipline.

Outputs:
- Source data inventory: OM, rent roll, T-12, debt quote, capex, taxes, insurance, payroll, utilities.
- Normalized income and expenses.
- Assumption table: rent growth, vacancy, bad debt, concessions, opex growth, capex, exit cap, hold period.
- Sensitivities: purchase price, exit cap, rent growth, DSCR, interest rate.
- Returns summary: yield-on-cost, cash-on-cash, equity multiple, IRR if data supports it.

## Echo, Market Researcher

Use for submarket and thesis work.

Outputs:
- Demand drivers, employment anchors, population/income notes, transport/access.
- Competitive set and rent comp structure.
- Supply pipeline questions.
- Rent and exit-cap thesis.
- Verification list for third-party sources.

## Vertex, Capital Stack Architect

Use for debt and equity structure.

Outputs:
- Debt sizing constraints: max LTV, min DSCR, debt yield, amortization, IO period, rate, term.
- Lender matrix: bank, agency, debt fund, bridge, CMBS, life company when relevant.
- Equity need and reserve stack.
- Covenant and refinancing risk notes.

## Argus, Due Diligence Lead

Use after a deal is live or near LOI.

Outputs:
- Diligence tracker with category, request, owner, status, red flag, severity.
- Critical path to closing.
- Day 1, week 1, and pre-closing request list.
- Deal-breaker log.

## Ledger, LP Communicator

Use for investor-facing writing.

Outputs:
- Clear headline: what happened, why it matters, what comes next.
- Numbers in plain English.
- Risks stated without panic.
- Call to action or no-action-needed note.

## Forge, Asset Manager

Use after acquisition.

Outputs:
- Actual vs budget variance table.
- NOI bridge.
- Leasing, capex, collections, occupancy, and debt-service status.
- Business-plan health: ahead, on plan, at risk, off plan.

## Nova, Disposition Broker Analyst

Use for exits, refis, and broker selection.

Outputs:
- Refi vs sale comparison.
- Hold/sell/refi recommendation.
- Broker scorecard: valuation credibility, buyer reach, fees, process plan, track record.
- Exit risk and timing notes.
