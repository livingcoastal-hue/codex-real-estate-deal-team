# Deal Screening

## Initial Score

Score each category 1-5, then give a short reason.

| Category | Weight |
| --- | ---: |
| Strategy fit | 20% |
| Market quality | 20% |
| Basis / price | 20% |
| NOI durability | 15% |
| Upside clarity | 10% |
| Financing feasibility | 10% |
| Execution risk | 5% |

Decision guide:
- `Pursue`: weighted score >= 4.0 and no fatal red flags.
- `Watch`: 3.2-3.9, missing inputs, or price likely too high but asset is relevant.
- `Pass`: < 3.2 or a fatal mismatch.
- `Needs Inputs`: not enough factual data for a fair call.

## Minimum Intake Fields

- Asset name and address
- Property type
- Units or rentable SF
- Year built / renovated
- Occupancy
- Asking price or guidance
- Current NOI or T-12 NOI
- In-place rents and market rent claim
- Capex need
- Debt assumptions or financing target
- Business plan
- Deadline and process stage

## Red Flags

- Unexplained NOI adjustments
- Tax reassessment not modeled
- Insurance reset ignored
- Occupancy cliff or major tenant rollover
- Capex hidden below the line
- Rent comps not truly comparable
- Debt proceeds require aggressive NOI
- Exit cap tighter than going-in cap without support
- Sponsor plan depends on several unverified assumptions at once
